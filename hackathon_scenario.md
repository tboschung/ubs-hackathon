# Hackathon Scenario
**Scenario 1**: Ticket intelligence and theme detection
helping risk teams understand what's really going wrong

**The challenge**: Risk and operations teams receive high volumes of incidents, escalations and operational-risk issues. Pattern identification is still manual and slow.


Create a lightweight Al prototype that:

- Clusters tickets into meaningful themes
- Detect emerging patterns and recurring issues
- Surfaces potential root causes
- Explains the key insights to a risk/operations user

Demo outputs:

Show a user going from raw tickets identified themes emerging patterns → actionable
insights. 
- "Spike in login failures after a mobile update"
- "Recurring FX issue in APAC"
